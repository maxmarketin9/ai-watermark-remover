Capabilities & limits

Images are re-encoded using Canvas. Audio is decoded with Web Audio and exported as PCM WAV. Text can be Unicode-normalized and cleaned of selected invisible formatting characters. Video support depends on the browser and is currently inspected and copied as a research baseline rather than claiming watermark removal.

This application does not forge provenance, software signatures, or C2PA credentials. It cannot guarantee removal of SynthID or proprietary invisible watermarks.
